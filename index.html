Know the Scam

The eight frauds reported most often. Flip a card to see how it works and what to do.

⚠ HIGH RISK
🎭

Digital Arrest

डिजिटल अरेस्ट
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

Callers pose as Police, CBI, Customs or ED on video call, claim a parcel/case in your name and “arrest” you online until you transfer money.

WHAT TO DO

No agency arrests on video call. Disconnect, never transfer, call 1930.

⚠ HIGH RISK
📈

Fake Trading / Investment

फ़र्ज़ी ट्रेडिंग / निवेश
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

WhatsApp/Telegram “stock tips” groups and fake apps show huge profits, then demand fees or taxes before you can withdraw.

WHAT TO DO

Invest only via SEBI-registered brokers. Huge guaranteed returns = fraud.

⚠ HIGH RISK
💼

Task / Part-time Job

टास्क / पार्ट-टाइम जॉब
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

You are paid small amounts for likes or reviews, then asked to “invest” in prepaid tasks that never pay back.

WHAT TO DO

Real jobs never ask you to pay. Exit the group and report.

⚠ HIGH RISK
🏦

KYC / Bank Update

KYC / बैंक अपडेट
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

SMS or call says your account or SIM will be blocked; a link or app captures your card, OTP or net-banking login.

WHAT TO DO

Banks never ask OTP/PIN. Update KYC only at the branch or official app.

⚠ HIGH RISK
📞

Fake Customer Care

फ़र्ज़ी कस्टमर केयर
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

Fraud numbers placed in search results ask you to install a screen-sharing app or pay a small “refund fee”.

WHAT TO DO

Take helpline numbers only from the official website or app.

⚠ HIGH RISK
📹

Sextortion

सेक्सटॉर्शन
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

A video call from a stranger is recorded and morphed; the victim is threatened and blackmailed for money.

WHAT TO DO

Do not pay. Block, keep evidence, report on cybercrime.gov.in.

⚠ HIGH RISK
💸

Instant Loan Apps

इंस्टेंट लोन ऐप
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

Easy loan apps take contacts and photo access, charge huge interest and harass family with morphed images.

WHAT TO DO

Borrow only from RBI-regulated lenders. Deny contact/gallery access.

⚠ HIGH RISK
📲

APK / Bill Update

APK / बिजली बिल
↻ Hover or tap to flip
HOW IT WORKS

“Electricity will be cut tonight” or “Wedding invite.apk” — the app forwards your SMS and OTPs to fraudsters.

WHAT TO DO

Never install APK files from WhatsApp. Use Play Store only.

From the IT industry to cyber policing

Before joining Uttar Pradesh Police, I worked in telecom and IT with Vodafone Idea, Tech Mahindra and HCL Tech. That experience now powers my investigations — understanding how networks, devices, apps and payment systems really work.

      Frequently asked questions

      Common questions after a cyber fraud.

      What happens after I call 1930?

      Your complaint is registered and the banks/wallets involved are alerted so the fraud amount can be put on hold. You receive an acknowledgement number — keep it safe.

      Will I get my money back?

      If the amount was held in time, it can be refunded after due legal process through the court/bank. The faster you report, the better the chance.

      Do I need to visit a police station?

      First call 1930 and file on cybercrime.gov.in. The cyber police station or your local police station may contact you for further action.

      Can police arrest me on a video call?

      No. There is no such thing as a “digital arrest”. Disconnect such calls immediately and report on 1930.

      My social media account was hacked — what now?

      Use the platform's “recover account” option, change email and password, turn on 2-step verification, warn your friends, and report on cybercrime.gov.in.

      Someone made a fake profile in my name?

      Save the profile link and screenshots, report it to the platform as impersonation, and file on cybercrime.gov.in.
